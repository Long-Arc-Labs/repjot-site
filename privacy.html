Privacy Policy

Last updated August 13, 2026

The short version: your workout notes live on your device and, if you turn on iCloud Sync, in your own private iCloud. We have no servers, no accounts, and no way to read what you write. The only data that ever reaches us is anonymous usage statistics.

Who we are

Repjot is a workout journal app for iPhone made by Long Arc Labs. If you have any question about this policy, email us at mibragimov.dev@gmail.com.

Your notes

Everything you write in Repjot — workout notes, bodyweight entries, exercise history, streaks — is stored locally on your device. We do not operate accounts or servers, and your notes are never transmitted to us.

If you enable iCloud Sync in Settings, your notes are additionally stored in your personal, private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit) so they sync across your devices and survive a lost phone. That data is tied to your Apple Account, protected by Apple, and inaccessible to us. You can turn iCloud Sync off at any time in the app's Settings.

Analytics

To understand which features are used and keep the app working well, Repjot uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. It receives anonymous signals such as "a note was saved" or "onboarding was completed", along with basic device information (app version, iOS version, device model). Identifiers are anonymized before they reach TelemetryDeck's servers, and no signal ever contains the content of your notes. Nothing collected can be used to identify you, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

If Repjot offers optional in-app purchases, payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your name, card, or billing details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchase receipts; it receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and purchase history, not your identity.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, or email collection
  • No advertising and no ad trackers
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with data brokers
  • No reading, mining, or analyzing the content of your notes

Your data, your control

  • Export — Settings → Export your notes gives you a copy of everything you've written, any time.
  • Delete — deleting the app deletes all locally stored data. If iCloud Sync was on, you can remove the synced copy in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
  • Analytics — analytics contain no personal data, so there is nothing to link back to you or delete on request.

Children

Repjot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Repjot handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. Material changes will also be called out in the app's release notes.
