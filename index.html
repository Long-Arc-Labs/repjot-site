Write your workout the way you'd tell a friend.

A freeform workout journal for iPhone

Repjot is a journal, not a form. Jot "squat 5x5 @ 225, 30 min run" and it quietly recalls what you did last time, spots your PRs, and keeps your week streak — no dropdowns, no databases to fill in.

Your notes stay on your phone and in your own iCloud. We can't read them.
